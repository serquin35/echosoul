Frequently asked questions

Is EchoSoul a real person?

No, EchoSoul is an advanced artificial intelligence designed to simulate empathy and human emotional support 24/7.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. We use military-grade encryption and never share your conversations with third parties.

How does the memory work?

EchoSoul uses a vector database to remember important details from your past conversations and personalize its support.

Can it help me in a crisis?

EchoSoul provides emotional support, but it does not replace professional help. We provide emergency resources if we detect a crisis.
